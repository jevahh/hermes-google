Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-11-13

1. Introduction
This app ("Hermes") is a personal assistant that manages your Google Calendar events.
It is a personal automation project, not a commercial service.

2. Data We Collect
Calendar events (event title, date/time) — read only from your Google Calendar to create reminders.
No other personal data — we do not collect contacts, messages, files, or location.
No tracking — we do not use cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers.
3. Data Usage
Calendar events are used solely to create and manage your reminders. They are not
stored permanently by this app and are not shared with any third party.
